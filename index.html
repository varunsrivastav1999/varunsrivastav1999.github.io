About

Machines and code, both

I'm a Mechanical Engineer turned Software Engineer at Taikisha Engineering India, where I've spent 3+ years building the software layer of automotive and manufacturing plants.

My work connects the shop floor to the web: talking to Siemens, Allen-Bradley and Mitsubishi PLCs, streaming live data through Django, Redis and WebSockets, and showing it in Vue.js dashboards that operators rely on around the clock.

Lately I've been adding AI to that stack, shipping a RAG assistant that lets operators ask questions of machine manuals and maintenance logs in plain language.
